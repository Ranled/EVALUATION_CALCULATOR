1. Formula for Weighted Score & Weighted Mean

For each indicator or criterion with frequencies $f_1, f_2, f_3, f_4, f_5$ corresponding to Likert scores $x \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$:

Weighted Score = (f5 × 5) + (f4 × 4) + (f3 × 3) + (f2 × 2) + (f1 × 1)
Weighted Mean = Σ(f × x) / N

Example from your Functional Suitability table (N = 50):
Functional Completeness: $f = (10\text{ at }5,\; 33\text{ at }4,\; 7\text{ at }3,\; 0\text{ at }2,\; 0\text{ at }1)$
$\text{Weighted Score} = (0\times1)+(0\times2)+(7\times3)+(33\times4)+(10\times5) = 203$
$\text{Weighted Mean} = 203 / 50 = \mathbf{4.06}$ (Satisfied)

2. Total Weighted Score & Criterion / General Average

To solve the Total Weighted Score (Sum of Weighted Means) and Total Weighted Mean across $k$ indicators or sub-criteria:

Total Weighted Score (Sum of WMs) = WM_1 + WM_2 + ... + WM_k
Total Weighted Mean (General Average) = (Σ WM) / k

Example from your Functional Suitability table:
$\text{Sum of Weighted Means} = 4.06 + 3.98 + 3.64 = \mathbf{11.68}$
$\text{Total Weighted Mean} = 11.68 / 3 = \mathbf{3.8933 \approx 3.89}$ (Satisfied)
